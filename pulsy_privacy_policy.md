# Privacy Policy for Pulsy (Scientific Research Project)

**Effective Date:** April 26, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Pulsy ("the App") is a non-commercial scientific research project developed for educational purposes within the framework of data analytics and financial market research. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user information, specifically in relation to the integration with the Dropbox API.

## 2. Data Collection and Usage
Pulsy is designed with a focus on privacy:
* **No Personal Data:** The App does not collect or store personal identification information (names, emails, or phone numbers) on its own servers.
* **No Data Selling:** We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties.

## 3. Dropbox Integration
Pulsy uses the Dropbox API to provide cloud synchronization features for analytical reports.
* **File Access:** The App only accesses a dedicated folder (e.g., `/Apps/Pulsy`) to save and load JSON configuration files and market reports.
* **Authentication:** All authentication is handled securely via Dropbox. Pulsy does not store or see your Dropbox credentials.

## 4. Third-Party Data Sources
The App fetches public market data from providers like CoinGecko and Coinalyze. These services do not receive any personal information from Pulsy users.

## 5. User Rights and Data Control
Users have full control over their data:
* You can revoke Dropbox access at any time through the App settings.
* You can manually delete any data stored by the App directly from your Dropbox account.
